FLAK HEAVEN — closed test (Windows)
===================================

Thanks for agreeing to test! This is an early prototype of an air-combat roguelike:
graphics, sound and balance are temporary. We value your honest impressions —
especially what is boring, unclear or annoying.

LAUNCH
------
1. Unzip the archive into any folder (for example, the desktop).
2. Run FlakHeaven.exe.
3. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC".
   That's because the test build has no paid code signature.
   Click "More info" → "Run anyway".
   If your antivirus complains, add the file to exclusions or contact us.
   How to check the file is safe: SAFETY_NOTE_en.txt.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 64-bit, any graphics card with OpenGL 3.3
(integrated is fine), 4 GB RAM. Mouse and keyboard; a gamepad works only in combat (menus and hangar — mouse).
No installation needed. To remove the game, delete the folder.

WHAT TO TRY (30–60 minutes)
---------------------------
• The game picks its language from your system. To switch:
  Settings → Game → Language (Auto / Русский / English).
• On first launch the "How to play" screen opens — please read it
  (it's also available from the menu).
• Play 3–5 sorties. Try all three planes:
  "Wasp" (fighter), "Rook" (attacker), "Whale" (bomber).
• Try at least once to complete a synergy: at level 3 choose a specialization
  and take cards marked "▲ toward specialization".
• Between sorties visit the HANGAR and spend your parts.
• Try to survive until extraction.
• F9 switches the camera tilt; Settings → Graphics → Graphics quality
  lowers the load if the game stutters (it may also lower it by itself).

HOW TO SEND RESULTS
-------------------
After each sortie the results screen has a "SEND REPORT" button.
Click it, answer a few short questions and click "SEND" —
the sortie stats and the technical log are attached automatically.
A report after every sortie is not required, but it helps a lot.
The sortie stats themselves (without answers) are sent after every sortie
even if you don't send a report.
If you end a sortie early (pause → "Main menu"), the game offers a short
report before leaving: answer a couple of questions or click
"Exit without report".

If you're offline, the report is saved and sent with the next report
or on the next game launch.

What is sent: your answers, sortie stats, OS and graphics card name,
screen resolution, average FPS and the game's technical log. The game
does not collect your name, email or other personal data.

Fallback (if the button doesn't work): in the main menu click LOGS —
the game folder opens; send the organizer the playtest_log_*.csv file
and the logs folder. The folder is here:
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Flak Heaven - Prototype 3 (3D)

GENERAL SURVEY (at the end of the test, reply in a message to the organizer)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. How many sorties did you play, and which plane did you use most?
2. Was it clear what to do in the first 2–3 minutes? What was unclear?
3. What was the most exciting moment?
4. Where did it get boring or repetitive (roughly at what minute)?
5. Did you understand how specializations and synergies work? Did you complete one?
   Did you feel it in combat?
6. How hard was it? (1 — far too easy, 5 — far too hard) Why?
7. Was it clear where the damage was coming from?
8. After a sortie, did you want to start the next one right away? Why or why not?
9. Did you use the hangar? Were the upgrades noticeable?
10. What annoyed you the most (controls, UI, camera, ...)?
11. Any crashes, freezes or FPS drops? On what computer
    (graphics card, if you know)?
12. If the game came out on Steam, would you wishlist it? (yes/no/maybe)
13. What games does it remind you of?
14. Anything else you'd like to say:

Please don't share the build or screenshots publicly —
this is a closed test. Thank you!
