# Story key art in the cutscenes (step 11 of the Highlands story)

**Decision (Kanta, 01.11.2026):** «попробуй нагенерить красивые картинки в стиле комиксов через Figma AI», then
option 1 — art for every cutscene card — and «делай что нужно… сделай сразу охуенно». Spending: Figma AI credits of
Kanta's own Pro plan, approved by him in the thread.

## What
- 9 pictures, comic style (ink + halftone), 1536×864 (the models' ceiling; 2048 was requested and refused by all
  three models), made with `mcp__Figma__generate_image` (gpt-image-2.5-flare): intro and outro of ops 1–4 and the
  epilogue card. Files `game/art/story/<scene id>.jpg` (JPEG q92, 4 MB in all); PNG originals in the project files
  (`docs/story/comic/`) and the Figma file.
- `Cutscene.play` loads `art/story/<scene id>.jpg` when F1 `story_art` > 0 (default on) and the file exists; the
  variants of a scene share the id, so they share the picture. With a picture the model flight is not spawned.
- `HudCutscene` draws the picture first: `CutsceneKeys.art_rect` scales it to cover the screen and pushes in 7 % with
  a 2 % sideways drift over the scene (smooth-step, never shows an edge; unit-tested), then a soft dark gradient above
  the lower bar so the title reads on any picture; the epilogue card dims it to 0.8 under its captions. Letterbox,
  captions, skip hint and fades are unchanged.
- Fallback: F1 `story_art` = 0, a missing file or the `--cutscene` test scene → the live fly-over as before.
