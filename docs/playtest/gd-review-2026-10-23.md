Kanta's task of 2026-10-23: review everything we have (decision log, backlog, tester feedback, sortie logs, `tuning.gd`) with the game designer skill and give a diagnosis with priorities for the Next Fest demo. No code was changed.

**Caveats.**

## 1. Summary

1. **We have two different games.** A maxed player deals 1000–2000× more damage at 15:00 than at minute 1 and is invulnerable after 8:00. A new player grows 30–60× and struggles after 10:00. Combat pressure (enemy HP adjustment) only kicks in for the maxed player and hits its ×4 cap.
2. **The most dangerous point of a sortie is the first ace (5:00–7:00)**, plus minutes 3–4. That is where humans die, and where the bot drops: in 8 of 11 runs it had ≤ 38 % HP at minute 6. The ace fight lasts 30–140 s although it is designed for 14 s of player damage.
3. **XP comes fast from the start.** Level 22–31 at 5:00 for everyone (bot 16–26). In minutes 2–4 a card choice every 6–12 s. For a strong player useful cards run out around level 60 (by 10:00 in s10).
4. **Air-to-air missiles are nearly everyone's main weapon**: 21–47 % of damage for «Оса» and «Грач». «Кит» bombs without modernization give 1.5–9 % of damage: the class's signature weapon does not show.
5. **A strong player heals more than he takes** (healed / taken = 1.07–1.35 in all of Kanta's long runs); a new player 0.5–0.9.

Main takeaway for the demo: **measure and fix balance on the demo player profile** (plane level 1, hangar up to level 2, crew up to level 5), not on a maxed one. The demo player is closer to the bot in section 3 than to Kanta's runs.

## 2. What already delivers the fun

- **Power growth is felt**: from a pair of guns to a sky full of missiles and synergies. Damage per minute grows ×30–60 even for the bot, hundreds of times for a human. That is the core of the genre — do not break it.
- **Synergies and modernizations get built.** EX-05: all 9 modernizations by minute 7–10. Logs show 2–4 synergies per sortie; players go for them on their own.
- **Activities and class tasks give a goal.** The bot completed the «Грач» task by minute 5–12 and the «Кит» task in all 4 runs («Оса» — no, see 4.6).
- **The end of a strong sortie is fireworks**: Kanta finished at 80–100 % HP with millions of damage. Fun, but without a threat it quickly gets boring — that is what S9-15 and S10-13 are about.

What it shows:
- **Combat pressure never kicks in for a new player.** The enemy HP multiplier stays 1.0 the whole sortie, except one «Грач» (up to ×2.8). So all of a new player's difficulty is base HP and `hp_ramp` (×4.2 by 15:00, `spawner.gd:530`) plus enemy damage growing after 7:00 up to ×2 (`dmg_ramp`).
- **The first ace is an HP pit.** The ace fight lasts 31–139 s (target: 14 s of player damage, `BOSS_TTK`). The bot hits an agile target poorly, so check the number on a human — but the shape matches humans: deaths at 5:41–6:47 in five sheet runs.
- **After 10:00 it is hard for a new player.** Most "would die" are in minutes 11–15: enemy damage grows while repair after 10:00 is cut (EX-10). Guess: a human dodges better than the bot, so the gap is smaller, but the sign is the same.
- **XP after S10-13**: level 35–47 at 15:00 (Kanta had 83 before the change). The curve target is ~level 35 by 15:00 (`main.gd:359`): almost reached for a new player; check the strong player in the next build.
- **Damage by class is similar** (11–80 k per minute at minute 15). «Оса»: A2A missiles and «Карусель». «Грач»: thermobaric, rockets, wingmen. «Кит»: gun, wingmen and bombs (bombs 3–17 %, with «Огненный ковёр» 50 %).
- **«Оса» class task** (shoot down 4 bombers before 12:00): the bot never completed it (0–1 of 4), although it did in the EX-07 autotest of 2026-10-22. Guess: the bot does not attack from the side, and a lone «Оса» against a wedge with front armor (×0.5) is too slow. Check with a human.

## 4. Problems by system

### 4.1 Combat pressure and power growth (layer "build in a run")
- **Rule now** (log 2026-10-18, S6-01): new enemies' HP ×1…×4, growing no faster than ×1.1 per 5 s; after the cap wave density grows to ×1.5. Target: 12 s time-to-kill at the start, 15.6 s by 14:00.
- **Data.** For a maxed player the multiplier hits ×4 by minute 6–10 (s7, s8, s10). Damage per minute grows ×1000–2000 (s10 «Оса»: 585 → 1.3 M). Enemy HP, together with `hp_ramp`, grows up to ~×17. Enemies die before they arrive.
- **Root cause.** Player power stacks multiplicatively: weapon levels, modules, synergies, mastery, and the «Карусель» / «Волчья стая» chains (×1.2 per relaunch). The enemy adjustment is capped. Aces are scaled to player damage (`spawner.gd:52`), so any player power looks the same to an ace.
- **Not critical for the demo** (guess: a demo player with the hangar capped at level 2 is closer to the bot, whose pressure never kicked in). Important for the full game: a strong player at the end of a sortie needs threats to dodge, not thicker enemies.

### 4.2 Danger curve
- **Humans** (12 deaths in the sheet): 3:25–4:13 — 4 times, 5:41–6:47 (first ace) — 5 times, 10:15–15:56 — 3 times.
- **Maxed player**: HP drops to 20–50 % in minutes 2–3, then 90–100 % after 8:00.
- **New player (bot)**: a pit at minute 6, then again in minutes 11–15.
- **Kanta's principle**: "not overcome, but enjoy". An early death at minute 3–4 is the worst moment for the demo: the player has built nothing yet and leaves. Most damage in those minutes comes from "fighter guns" (drones and interceptors): about half or more of damage taken (47–65 % in the early s9 deaths by `taken_kind`).

### 4.3 Aces
- **Rule now** (EX-06, S9-24): ace HP = player damage over 14 / 24 / 28 s, shield ×0.35 against "wrong" damage.
- **Data.** The first ace lives 30–140 s against the bot, 9–75 s against humans. For «Кит» in s9 aces dealt 28–38 % of all damage taken (370–490 of ~1300).
- The first ace is the first "exam" of a sortie and now also the main cause of death. For the demo it should be **spectacular but forgiving**: a dangerous attack with a clear warning, not a long duel.

### 4.4 XP and cards
- **Curve** `xp_need = 4 + 2(L−1) + 0.07(L−1)²` is designed for ~level 35 by 15:00. At 5:00 everyone is already at 22–31.
- **Minutes 2–4**: 5–10 levels per minute, a choice every 6–12 s. Guess: often for the genre, combat is broken by pauses — but genre players are used to frequent early cards, so check the feel first, do not change yet.
- **S10-13** (kill XP ×0.5 after 7:00) is in the code. By the autotest it gives level 35–47 at 15:00 for a new player; check the strong player in the next build. Target: last core cards by minute 12–13.
- **S9-15** (either/or mastery after level 35) adds choice at the top. Check in the build whether it lasts to 15:00.

### 4.5 Weapons
- **A2A missiles** are the top weapon for «Оса» (24–47 %) and for «Грач» in 3 of 4 long runs (21–35 %); before 2026-10-18 also for «Кит» (44 % in s4).
- **«Кит» bombs** (its +20 % signature) without modernization: 1.5–9 % of damage for humans, 3–17 % for the bot; with «Огненный ковёр» 50 %. Bombs hit only ground targets, and there are few ground targets nearby. The skill's rule: **buff the weak, do not cut the popular** — pull bombs up, leave A2A missiles alone.
- The top damage comes from synergies («Карусель», «Волчья стая», «Цепная реакция», «Термобарика»). That is fine: they are the reward for a build. Only the chains with ×1.2 growth per relaunch are risky (S10-13 option 2, not chosen by Kanta).

### 4.6 Classes
- **«Оса»** is the most fragile: new-player HP 120–172 against 160–369 for «Грач» and 422–730 for «Кит». Among humans it dies earliest (3:26, 3:32, 4:13). The "maneuver" role does not protect yet: damage comes from crowd guns, not from missiles you dodge with a maneuver.
- **«Грач»** is the all-rounder and the strongest by damage in the logs; little difference from «Оса» — same main damage (A2A missiles) plus rockets and turrets.
- **«Кит»** is tough, but its "bombs" role does not show in the numbers (4.5). In s9 Kanta's «Кит» died to aces (S9-24).
- **«Оса» task** (4 bombers before 12:00) — the bot never completed it. Check with a human; if a human also fails, ease the condition rather than punish.

### 4.7 Meta progression (hangar, crew, plane level 2)
- Almost all sheet runs were played with nodes 5/5. **We do not know** what the demo player's game feels like (cap level 2, crew up to level 5).
- The "new ↔ maxed" gap is huge: damage at 5:00 — bot ~6 k per minute, Kanta in s10 31 k. Good for the feeling of growth between sorties, but balance tuned for the maxed player breaks the new one, and vice versa.
- Repair: a maxed player heals more than he takes, a new player 0.5–0.9. EX-10 cut repair after 10:00 for everyone — this hurts the new player more and barely touches the maxed one (guess, check in the build).

## 5. Priorities for the Next Fest demo

1. **Demo player profile in the autotest** (a flag like `--profile=demo`: plane level 1, nodes level 2, crew level 5) plus a short table of sortie metrics: level at 5 / 10 / 15:00, lowest HP by minute, ace fight duration, "would die", repair / taken. This is the basis of the R-10 method (`docs/balance-method.md`); without it every balance change is a guess from one sortie. Size S, combat code untouched.
2. **First ace (5:00) and minutes 3–4** — the main place of death. After measuring on the demo profile, one change per build (decision GD-02).
3. **«Кит» bombs** — pull up so the signature weapon shows in the numbers (decision GD-03).
4. **Late combat for a strong player** (threats instead of thickness) — after the demo; demo players probably will not get there (guess, check with item 1).

No new balance changes in this build: S10-13 (XP) and EX-10 (repair) are already waiting for checks, and "one change at a time" does not allow stacking more on top.
